ON THE BRINK

TACTICAL GROUND COMBAT DOCTRINE

Authorized Publication // Ver 0.3 // Sector 7

> DOCTRINAL AMENDMENTS (VER 0.3)

  • Unified Mechanics: All tests resolved via "Roll Over" D10 logic.
  • Standardized AP: 2 Action Points generated per activation; most actions cost 1 AP.
  • Command Logic: Activation Tokens are generated per surviving unit Leader.
  • Engagement: Melee is simultaneous; losers must pass LDR or retreat.
  • Structural Integrity: Hard Cover utilize ARM values; standard saves are ignored.
  • Stress Impact: Removed Pinned status; Stress provides cumulative LDR penalties.
  • Momentum Logic: Defined fixed attrition costs (-1 for Infantry, -2 for Vehicles).

On the Brink is a wargame of high-stakes tactical combat where momentum shifts with every decision. Commanders must manage squad stress and casualties to break enemy resolve before their own forces collapse.

1. CORE CONCEPTS

1.1 THE UNIFIED DICE MECHANIC

To maintain high operational tempo, all dice rolls in On the Brink are "Roll Over". When resolving any test—attacking, Survival Saves, or Leadership—roll a D10. Success is achieved by rolling equal to or higher than the specified Target Number (TN).

1.2 MOMENTUM (VICTORY CONDITIONS)

Momentum represents your army's overall operational tempo, morale, and will to fight. Both players typically begin the game with 10 Momentum.

  • Gaining Momentum: Securing objectives and destroying high-value enemy units adds Momentum.
  • Losing Momentum: Taking casualties, failing critical Leadership tests, and losing objectives reduces your Momentum.
  • Attrition Baseline: Whenever an Infantry Squad is completely destroyed, that player loses -1 Momentum. When a Vehicle is destroyed, that player loses -2 Momentum.
  • Victory Condition: If a player's Momentum reaches 0, their army breaks and they immediately lose the game. If the game reaches the turn limit, the player with the highest Momentum wins.

1.3 SQUAD INTEGRITY

Infantry operate in rigid Squads. Each squad features a designated Leader model.

  • A Squad activates together, moves together, and tracks its Stress Tokens as a single cohesive unit.
  • Cohesion: All models in a Squad must end their movement within 2" of at least one other model in their Squad.
  • Note: To speed up gameplay, Squads may NOT dynamically split into smaller elements mid-game.

2. THE BATTLE TURN

Operations are divided into three distinct phases:

I. COMMAND PHASE

  1. Activation Tokens: Each player surveys their forces. For every functional unit on the table (a unit that still has a living Leader model), the player receives 1 Activation Token.
  2. Initiative: Both players roll a D10. The highest roller gains Initiative and decides who will take the first turn in the Active Phase.

II. ACTIVE PHASE

Players alternate taking turns, starting with the player who won Initiative. On your turn, you may spend 1 Activation Token to activate one unit or Pass. These tokens can be spent on any unit, meaning you may choose to activate the same unit multiple times.

  • First Activation: When a unit is activated for the first time in a Game Turn, it automatically generates exactly 2 Action Points (AP).
  • Multiple Activations (Pushing): You may spend an Activation Token to activate a unit that has already been activated this turn. To do so, the unit must immediately take a Leadership (LDR) test. (Note: Stress Token penalties apply to this roll).
    • Success: The unit generates 2 AP and takes its activation normally.
    • Failure: The unit generates 0 AP, its activation ends immediately, and it automatically gains the Gone to Ground status.
  • Passing & End of Phase: If a player chooses not to spend a token, they may Pass. If a player runs out of Activation Tokens, they must Pass. Once both players run out of tokens (or both players consecutively Pass), the Active Phase ends. Any remaining unspent tokens are discarded.

III. END PHASE

  1. Morale & Leadership: Units with high levels of Stress must take Leadership Tests.
  2. Rally: Surviving units remove 1 Stress Token.
  3. Check Victory: Evaluate Momentum. If neither player has hit 0, begin a new Game Turn.

3. TACTICAL ACTIONS

When a unit activates successfully, it generates 2 Action Points (AP). All actions cost 1 AP.
RESTRICTION: A unit may not perform the same action twice during a single activation, with the sole exception of the Move action, which may be repeated (e.g., Move + Move).

AUTHORIZED ACTIONS [COST: 1 AP]

  • MOVE (1 AP): Travel up to MOV distance. May be performed twice per activation.
  • DASH (1 AP): Travel MOV + 2 inches. Incurs 1 Stress Token.
  • FIRE WEAPON (1 AP): Resolve ranged attack. Prohibited if Engaged.
  • TAKE COVER (1 AP): Gain Gone to Ground status (+1 SRV save).
  • ASSAULT (1 AP): Initiate Melee (requires models to be within 1" of enemy).
  • FALL BACK (1 AP): Move away from Engagement. Incurs 1 Stress. Enemy may make a free Reaction Attack.
  • SPECIAL (1 AP): Clear jams, Claim objectives, or Scenario-specific tasks.

3.1 EXECUTING MOVEMENT & ENGAGEMENT

Follow these steps to ensure rapid deployment:

  1. Select the unit leader model.
  2. Measure and move the leader model up to its movement value (or +2" if Dashing).
  3. Pick up the remaining models and place them in their new positions within Cohesion (2").

Engagement Range: If a unit ends its movement with any of its models within 1 inch of an enemy model, both units are now considered Engaged in Close Combat. Engaged units cannot perform the Fire Weapon action.

3.2 REACTIONS

When an inactive unit is targeted by an enemy attack, it may immediately choose to spend 1 Stress Token to declare a Reaction Attack.

  • Simultaneous Resolution: Reaction Fire attacks are considered to happen at the exact same time. Both players roll their attacks, saves, and damage simultaneously.
  • Casualty Rule: If a model is reduced to 0 health during a simultaneous exchange, it still resolves its attack completely before being removed from the table as a casualty.

4. STATUS EFFECTS & MARKERS

Units will accrue markers that dictate their condition and effectiveness throughout the battle.

  • Stress Tokens: Accrued by being shot at, dashing, or failing combat.
    • Training Penalty: Having at least one Stress Token imposes a flat -1 penalty to all Attack rolls.
    • Leadership Penalty: Imposes a cumulative -1 penalty to Leadership tests for each Stress Token the unit currently has.
  • Gone to Ground: Gained by performing the Take Cover action or by failing a Multiple Activation Leadership test. The unit gains a +1 bonus to their Survival (SRV) save. This status is immediately lost if the unit performs a Move, Dash, or Fall Back action, or if they are pushed back in Close Combat.
  • Smoke: Placed by specific weapons or abilities. Smoke markers create a dense cloud (usually 3"). Line of Sight cannot be drawn through a smoke cloud. Dissipates during the End Phase of the following turn.
  • Engaged: Applied automatically when within 1" of an enemy. Engaged units cannot fire ranged weapons and must either Assault or Fall Back.

5. RESOLVING RANGED COMBAT

Combat is resolved in three distinct steps:

STEP 1: ROLL TO HIT

Measure range and Line of Sight. Roll a D10 for each attack generated.

  • Target Number: The Firer’s Training (TRN) stat.
  • Modifiers: Apply modifiers such as Target in Cover (-1) or Firing at close range (+1).

STEP 2: ROLL SAVES

The type of save depends on the target classification:

  • Infantry (Survival Save): If in the open or light cover, roll D10 equal to or higher than Survival (SRV). Weapon Penetration (Pen) has NO effect on SRV saves. Success = no damage. Failure = 1 Wound.
  • Exception: Infantry inside Buildings or behind Hard Cover do not roll SRV (See Section 7).
  • Vehicles (Armor Save): Vehicles do not roll SRV. The Attacker rolls an Anti-Tank Test: D10 + Weapon Penetration (Pen) value. If the total equals or exceeds Vehicle Armor (ARM) on the hit facing, the vehicle takes damage.

STEP 3: CASUALTIES & STRESS

Remove one infantry model from the squad, or mark one structural point on a vehicle, for every unsaved Wound.
Apply Stress: Regardless of saves, any unit successfully Hit by an attack immediately takes 1 Stress Token for being under fire.

6. RESOLVING CLOSE COMBAT

Close Combat is swift, simultaneous, and decisive.

STEP 1: SIMULTANEOUS STRIKES

There is no attacker advantage. Both units fight simultaneously. Each player rolls 1 D10 for every model in their unit within 1" of an enemy. Target Number is the unit's TRN. Cover modifiers are ignored.

STEP 2 & 3: SAVES & ROUTING

For every successful Hit, players roll SRV saves for their units as normal. After Wounds are applied, determine the winner:

  • The unit that suffered the most unsaved Wounds is the loser.
  • Leadership Test: The losing unit must immediately take a Leadership (LDR) test (applying stress penalties).
    • Pass: Hold ground, remain Engaged.
    • Fail: Take 1 Stress Token and are forcibly pushed back 2" out of Engagement Range.
  • Ties: If both sides suffered equal Wounds, neither side is pushed back.

7. TERRAIN AND COVER

7.1 MOVEMENT TERRAIN

  • Open Ground: Normal movement rules apply.
  • Difficult Terrain: Dense woods, mud, rubble. Movement value is halved for that action (e.g., 6" becomes 3").
  • Obstacles: Low walls, fences. It costs a unit 2 inches of movement to cross an obstacle.

7.2 COVER TERRAIN

  • Light Cover: Foliage, wooden fences, car hulls. Imposes a -1 penalty to enemy Ranged Hit rolls if obscured.
  • Hard Cover & Buildings: Concrete bunkers, thick brick walls. Infantry in these positions act exactly like armored vehicles when attacked.
    • Building/Cover ARM: Structures provide a flat Armor Value (e.g., ARM 6).
    • Resolution: Defenders DO NOT roll SRV. Attacker rolls Anti-Tank (D10 + Pen) against the structure's ARM for EACH hit.
    • Casualties: Every roll that equals or exceeds the ARM value removes one infantry model from the garrisoning squad.
  • Elevation: Firing from an elevated position (at least 3" higher) ignores the target's Light Cover modifier.

8. OPERATIONS LOGISTICS

Objectives are captured by spending 1 AP (Special Action) while in control (within 3" with no enemy present).

MOMENTUM SHIFTS:
- CLAIM NEUTRAL: +2 MOMENTUM
- STEAL ENEMY: +2 TO SELF / -2 TO ENEMY

9. MISSION FILES

OP 01: FRONTLINE CLASH

Standard meeting engagement. Symmetrical objectives along the center axis.

  • Objectives: Center point and 12" inward from left/right edges.
  • No Man's Land: Attempting to Claim the center objective triggers 1 Stress from sniper fire.
  • Attrition: Turns 3-5: Player with fewer objectives drains -1 Momentum.

OP 04: HOLD THE LINE

Garrison forces must defend localized intelligence from an encircling assault.

  • Dug In: Defender starts Gone to Ground.
  • Extraction: Extract intel via 1 AP Special Action (+4/-4 Momentum shift).

Refer to Section 9 of printed Manual for Operations 02, 03, 05, and 06.

10. SPECIAL RULES LEXICON

Special rules represent unique weapon capabilities and unit training. If a special rule contradicts the core doctrine, the special rule takes precedence.

Anti-Aircraft (AA)

Optimized for tracking high-speed airborne targets. Ignores all standard negative modifiers when firing at Aircraft or Helicopter units.

Armor Piercing

Specialized kinetic or chemical penetrators. A natural Hit roll of 10 always succeeds and penetrates Armor, regardless of the target's ARM value.

Arc Fire

Indirect ballistic trajectory. May target enemy units outside of Line of Sight, provided a friendly unit has LOS to the target. Attacks suffer a -1 penalty.

Blast (X)

High-explosive payload. A successful hit inflicts X Wounds on the target unit. The defender must roll Survival saves for each wound individually.

Entrench

Superior defensive drilling. When performing Take Cover, the unit gains an additional +1 SRV save (total +2) as long as they remain stationary.

Gun Port

Protected firing positions. Up to 2 embarked infantry models may perform Fire Weapon actions using the vehicle's hull as the LOS origin.

One Shot

Disposable weapon system. This weapon may only be discharged once per engagement.

Open Hatch

Exposed crew. If used, any Infantry firing at the vehicle in the following turn generates a Stress Token on a successful Hit, even if the weapon cannot penetrate ARM.

Reload (X)

Sustained fire limitation. After firing, the unit must spend X total Action Points on Special actions to "Reload" before firing again.

Smoke (X)

Visual obscuration. Instead of attacking, place an X-inch smoke cloud. Blocks all Line of Sight. Dissipates at the start of the next turn's End Phase.

Suppression

Psychological effect. Any unit successfully hit by this weapon immediately suffers 2 Stress Tokens instead of the standard 1.

Unreliable

Faulty mechanics. On a natural Hit roll of 1, the weapon jams. Must spend 1 AP on a Special action to clear before reuse.

Wire-Guided

Active guidance system. The unit cannot Move or Dash in the same activation it fires this weapon. Missed attacks may be rerolled.
