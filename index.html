0. FIELD MANUAL UPDATES (v0.3)

> DOCTRINAL AMENDMENTS

  • Unified Mechanics: All tests resolved via "Roll Over" D10 logic.
  • Standardized AP: 2 Action Points generated per activation; most actions cost 1 AP.
  • Command Logic: Activation Tokens are generated per surviving unit Leader.
  • Engagement: Melee is simultaneous; losers must pass LDR or retreat.
  • Hard Cover: Infantry in structural cover utilize ARM values; standard saves are ignored.
  • Vehicles: Specific rules for armor facings, weapon arcs, and line of sight measurement.

> VERSION 0.3 LOG

  • Momentum Integration: Scenarios now explicitly define starting Momentum and how specific objectives impact Momentum gains/losses.
  • Action Point Economy: Scenario actions (like gathering intel or rigging explosives) have been updated to utilize the new 1 AP "Special" action.
  • Updated Graphics & Interface: Scenario maps have been redesigned into clean tactical diagrams. The Theatre Strategic Map has been completely digitized into an interactive command display.
  • No D3 Rolls: Variable objective scoring and deployment distances have been converted to fixed values to maintain the game's fast pace.
  • Momentum Balancing: Established universal baseline for Momentum loss from casualties, capped Evacuation scoring, and added End Phase pressure to prevent stagnation.

1. CORE CONCEPTS

1.1 THE UNIFIED DICE MECHANIC

To maintain high operational tempo, all dice rolls in On the Brink are "Roll Over". When resolving any test—attacking, Survival Saves, or Leadership—roll a D10. Success is achieved by rolling equal to or higher than the specified Target Number (TN).

1.2 MOMENTUM (VICTORY CONDITIONS)

Momentum represents your army's overall operational tempo, morale, and will to fight. Both players typically begin the game with 10 Momentum.

  • Gaining Momentum: Securing objectives and destroying high-value enemy units adds Momentum.
  • Losing Momentum: Taking casualties, failing critical Leadership tests, and losing objectives reduces your Momentum.
  • Attrition Baseline: Destroyed Infantry Squads cost -1 Momentum. Destroyed Vehicles cost -2 Momentum.

1.3 SQUAD INTEGRITY

Infantry operate in rigid Squads led by a designated Leader model.

  • A Squad activates together, moves together, and tracks its Stress Tokens as a single cohesive unit.
  • Cohesion: All models in a Squad must end their movement within 2" of at least one other model in their Squad.

2. THE BATTLE TURN

Operations are divided into three distinct phases: Command, Active, and End Phase.

I. COMMAND PHASE

  1. Activation Tokens: Receive 1 Token for every functional unit on the table (with a living Leader).
  2. Initiative: Both players roll a D10. High roller gains Initiative for the turn.

II. ACTIVE PHASE

Players alternate spending 1 Activation Token to activate a unit.

  • First Activation: Automatically generates 2 Action Points (AP).
  • Pushing: Activating a unit again requires a Leadership (LDR) test. Success provides 2 AP; Failure ends the activation and the unit goes Gone to Ground.

3. TACTICAL ACTIONS

All actions cost 1 AP. A unit may not perform the same action twice, except for Move.

AUTHORIZED ACTIONS [COST: 1 AP]

  • MOVE (1 AP): Travel up to MOV distance. May be performed twice.
  • DASH (1 AP): Travel MOV + 2 inches. Incurs 1 Stress Token.
  • FIRE WEAPON (1 AP): Resolve ranged attack. Prohibited if Engaged.
  • TAKE COVER (1 AP): Gain Gone to Ground status (+1 SRV save).
  • ASSAULT (1 AP): Initiate Melee (requires models within 1" of enemy).
  • FALL BACK (1 AP): Move away from Engagement. Incurs 1 Stress.
  • SPECIAL (1 AP): Clear jams, Claim objectives, or Embark/Disembark.

4. VEHICLE OPERATIONS

4.1 ARMOR FACINGS & RESOLUTION

Vehicles track three armor values: Front, Side, and Rear. Attacks use the ARM value corresponding to the arc where the attacker's leader model is located.

Anti-Tank Test: Attacker rolls D10 + Weapon Pen. If Total ≥ ARM, the vehicle takes 1 structural Wound.

4.2 VEHICLE LINE OF SIGHT

  • Turreted Weapons: 360-degree field of fire measured from turret center.
  • Hull-Mounted: Measured from weapon barrel. Restricted to a 45-degree front arc.
  • Commander/Optics: LOS for spotting/Gone to Ground is measured from the primary optic/cupola.

4.3 TRANSPORTS & COVER

  • Embark/Disembark (1 AP): Requires unit to be within 1" of hull.
  • Operational Vehicles: Provide Hard Cover. Use the vehicle's Side ARM for hits against infantry behind it.
  • Wrecks: Destroyed vehicles provide Light Cover (-1 to hit).

5. RESOLVING COMBAT

Ranged: Roll to Hit (TRN) → Roll Save (SRV or ARM) → Remove Casualties & Apply Stress (+1 per successful Hit).

Melee: Simultaneous D10s (TRN) → SRV Saves → Loser (most Wounds) takes LDR test. Failure = Push back 2" and +1 Stress.

6. TERRAIN AND COVER

  • Light Cover: Foliage, car hulls, wrecks. Imposes -1 penalty to Hit rolls.
  • Hard Cover & Buildings: Infantry in structures act like armored vehicles. Defenders do not roll SRV; attackers must penetrate structure ARM.

8. OPERATIONS LOGISTICS

Objectives are captured by spending 1 AP (Special Action) while in control (within 3" with no enemy present).

MOMENTUM SHIFTS:
- CLAIM NEUTRAL: +2 MOMENTUM
- STEAL ENEMY: +2 TO SELF / -2 TO ENEMY

10. SPECIAL RULES LEXICON

Anti-Aircraft (AA)

Ignores negative modifiers vs Aircraft/Helicopters.

Armor Piercing

Natural 10 always penetrates Armor facings.

Arc Fire

May target out of LOS if spotted. -1 penalty to Hit.

Blast (X)

Successful hit inflicts X Wounds.

Entrench

+1 SRV bonus while Gone to Ground and stationary.

Gun Port

Embarked models may perform Fire actions from vehicle.

One Shot

System may only be discharged once per mission.

Open Hatch

Unit generates Stress if hit by small arms fire.

Reload (X)

Spend X AP on Special actions to reload.

Smoke (X)

Place X-inch cloud. Blocks Line of Sight.

Suppression

Successful hit inflicts 2 Stress Tokens.

Wire-Guided

Stationary fire only. Missed attacks may reroll.

14. OPERATIONAL FORCES

Current combat zone involves four distinct geopolitical entities (Post-Feb 1987). Identification and engagement protocols differ per faction.

NEW PRUSSIA (THE EMPIRE)

The epicenter of the current crisis. Following the mysterious "Iron Cross" PMC interventions in 1986, Emperor Friedrich-Wilhelm von Hohen-Strelitz revealed his hand on February 15, 1987. Claiming sovereignty over Germany, Sweden, Poland, and Scandinavia, the Restauratio Borussica is built on iron discipline and secret West German technology. Led by Gen-Major von der Schulenburg, they field advanced, highly disciplined forces emblazoned with the Geometric Black Eagle.

  • - HIGH TRN (4+) AND LDR (5+) STATS
  • - SPECIALIZED ADVANCED ARMOR
  • - EMPHASIS ON TECHNOLOGICAL SUPERIORITY

NATO (THE DECAPITATED WEST)

A coalition in absolute shock. Reeling from the nuclear destruction of Hamburg in '86, the West suffered a lethal blow in January 1987 when the Geneva Bombing killed the US President and British PM. Compounding this, their intelligence networks were entirely scrubbed from the inside by Prussian architect Elena von Zitzewitz-Moltke. Paralyzed and stretched thin without their central European foothold, they fight a desperate defensive war.

  • - VERSATILE INFANTRY SYSTEMS
  • - RELIANCE ON AIR SUPERIORITY (AA RULES)
  • - WIDE RANGE OF COMBINED ARMS

THE WARSAW PACT

The Soviet Union has descended into chaos. Following Marshal Volkov's military coup and Gorbachev's imprisonment, Volkov himself was assassinated in late January 1987. The resulting power vacuum has left the Eastern Bloc in open revolt. Despite the turmoil, the Pact remains a terrifying juggernaut. They rely on massed artillery and rugged, battle-tested armor to try and crush the Prussian uprising by sheer force of numbers.

  • - MASSED ARTILLERY (ARC FIRE / BLAST)
  • - HEAVY, RUGGED VEHICLE DESIGNS
  • - AGGRESSIVE ASSAULT DOCTRINE

BREAK AWAY REPUBLICS

Comprising Czechoslovakia, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Austria, Yugoslavia, and Albania, these nations have been caught between the Prussian blitz and Soviet internal collapse. Local commanders have seized control in the vacuum, prioritizing survival and independence. Using a mixture of stolen Warsaw Pact equipment, captured NATO gear, and improvised tactics, they wage a brutal, asymmetric war against anyone crossing their borders.

  • - AMBUSH TACTICS (GONE TO GROUND FOCUS)
  • - MIXED WEAPONRY (NATO / PACT HYBRIDS)
  • - GUERRILLA WARFARE EXPERTS

ENGAGEMENT PROTOCOL: THE OLD WORLD ORDER IS DEAD. ALL NON-ALIGNED FORCES ARE TO BE TREATED AS HOSTILES.
