ON THE BRINK

TACTICAL GROUND COMBAT DOCTRINE

Authorized Publication // Ver 0.3 // Sector 7

> DOCTRINAL AMENDMENTS (VER 0.3)

  • Unified Mechanics: All tests resolved via "Roll Over" D10 logic.
  • Standardized AP: 2 Action Points generated per activation; most actions cost 1 AP.
  • Command Logic: Activation Tokens are generated per surviving unit Leader.
  • Engagement: Melee is simultaneous; losers must pass LDR or retreat.
  • Structural Integrity: Hard Cover utilize ARM values; standard saves are ignored.
  • Stress Impact: Removed Pinned status; Stress provides cumulative LDR penalties.
  • Momentum Logic: Defined fixed attrition costs (-1 for Infantry, -2 for Vehicles).

On the Brink is a wargame of high-stakes tactical combat where momentum shifts with every decision. Commanders must manage squad stress and casualties to break enemy resolve before their own forces collapse.

1. CORE CONCEPTS

1.1 THE UNIFIED DICE MECHANIC

To maintain high operational tempo, all dice rolls in On the Brink are "Roll Over". When resolving any test—attacking, Survival Saves, or Leadership—roll a D10. Success is achieved by rolling equal to or higher than the specified Target Number (TN).

1.2 MOMENTUM (VICTORY CONDITIONS)

Momentum represents your army's overall operational tempo, morale, and will to fight. Both players typically begin the game with 10 Momentum.

  • Gaining Momentum: Securing objectives and destroying high-value enemy units adds Momentum.
  • Losing Momentum: Taking casualties, failing critical Leadership tests, and losing objectives reduces your Momentum.
  • Attrition Baseline: Whenever an Infantry Squad is completely destroyed, that player loses -1 Momentum. When a Vehicle is destroyed, that player loses -2 Momentum.
  • Victory Condition: If a player's Momentum reaches 0, their army breaks and they immediately lose the game. If the game reaches the turn limit, the player with the highest Momentum wins.

1.3 SQUAD INTEGRITY

Infantry operate in rigid Squads. Each squad features a designated Leader model.

  • A Squad activates together, moves together, and tracks its Stress Tokens as a single cohesive unit.
  • Cohesion: All models in a Squad must end their movement within 2" of at least one other model in their Squad.

2. THE BATTLE TURN

Operations are divided into three distinct phases:

I. COMMAND PHASE

  1. Activation Tokens: Each player surveys their forces. For every functional unit on the table (a unit that still has a living Leader model), the player receives 1 Activation Token.
  2. Initiative: Both players roll a D10. The highest roller gains Initiative and decides who will take the first turn in the Active Phase.

II. ACTIVE PHASE

Players alternate taking turns, starting with the player who won Initiative. On your turn, you may spend 1 Activation Token to activate one unit or Pass. These tokens can be spent on any unit, meaning you may choose to activate the same unit multiple times.

  • First Activation: When a unit is activated for the first time in a Game Turn, it automatically generates exactly 2 Action Points (AP).
  • Multiple Activations (Pushing): You may spend an Activation Token to activate a unit that has already been activated this turn. To do so, the unit must immediately take a Leadership (LDR) test.
    • Success: The unit generates 2 AP and takes its activation normally.
    • Failure: The unit generates 0 AP, its activation ends immediately, and it automatically gains the Gone to Ground status.

III. END PHASE

  1. Morale & Leadership: Units with high levels of Stress must take Leadership Tests.
  2. Rally: Surviving units remove 1 Stress Token.
  3. Check Victory: Evaluate Momentum. If neither player has hit 0, begin a new Game Turn.

3. TACTICAL ACTIONS

When a unit activates successfully, it generates 2 Action Points (AP). All actions cost 1 AP.
RESTRICTION: A unit may not perform the same action twice during a single activation, with the sole exception of the Move action.

AUTHORIZED ACTIONS [COST: 1 AP]

  • MOVE (1 AP): Travel up to MOV distance. May be performed twice per activation.
  • DASH (1 AP): Travel MOV + 2 inches. Incurs 1 Stress Token.
  • FIRE WEAPON (1 AP): Resolve ranged attack. Prohibited if Engaged.
  • TAKE COVER (1 AP): Gain Gone to Ground status (+1 SRV save).
  • ASSAULT (1 AP): Initiate Melee (requires models to be within 1" of enemy).
  • FALL BACK (1 AP): Move away from Engagement. Incurs 1 Stress. Enemy may make a free Reaction Attack.
  • SPECIAL (1 AP): Clear jams, Claim objectives, or Scenario-specific tasks.

3.1 EXECUTING MOVEMENT & ENGAGEMENT

Follow these steps to ensure rapid deployment:

  1. Select the unit leader model.
  2. Measure and move the leader model up to its movement value (or +2" if Dashing).
  3. Pick up the remaining models and place them in their new positions within Cohesion (2").

Engagement Range: If a unit ends its movement with any of its models within 1 inch of an enemy model, both units are now considered Engaged in Close Combat. Engaged units cannot perform the Fire Weapon action.

3.2 REACTIONS

When an inactive unit is targeted by an enemy attack, it may immediately choose to spend 1 Stress Token to declare a Reaction Attack.

  • Simultaneous Resolution: Reaction Fire attacks are considered to happen at the exact same time. Both players roll their attacks, saves, and damage simultaneously.
  • Casualty Rule: If a model is reduced to 0 health during a simultaneous exchange, it still resolves its attack completely before being removed from the table as a casualty.

4. STATUS EFFECTS & MARKERS

  • Stress Tokens: Accrued by being shot at, dashing, or failing combat.
    • Training Penalty: Having at least one Stress Token imposes a flat -1 penalty to all Attack rolls.
    • Leadership Penalty: Imposes a cumulative -1 penalty to Leadership tests for each Stress Token the unit currently has.
  • Gone to Ground: Gained by performing the Take Cover action or by failing a Multiple Activation Leadership test. The unit gains a +1 bonus to their Survival (SRV) save.
  • Smoke: Creates a dense cloud (usually 3"). Line of Sight cannot be drawn through a smoke cloud.
  • Engaged: Applied automatically when within 1" of an enemy.

5. RESOLVING RANGED COMBAT

1. To Hit: Roll D10. Target is TRN stat. Modifiers: Cover (-1), Point Blank (+1).
2. Saves: If hit, roll SRV. If failed, take 1 Wound. (Penetration only applies to Armor/Hard Cover).
3. Casualties: Remove models. Unit takes 1 Stress Token for being successfully hit.

6. RESOLVING CLOSE COMBAT

Melee is simultaneous. Both sides roll 1 D10 for every model within 1". Hits are resolved against TRN (Cover is ignored). Defender rolls SRV as normal. The loser (most wounds taken) must pass LDR or take 1 Stress and be pushed back 2".

7. TERRAIN AND COVER

  • Light Cover: Foliage, car hulls. Imposes a -1 penalty to enemy Ranged Hit rolls.
  • Hard Cover & Buildings: Infantry in these positions **act exactly like armored vehicles when attacked.**
    • Building/Cover ARM: Structures provide a flat Armor Value (e.g., ARM 6).
    • Resolution: Defenders DO NOT roll SRV. Attacker rolls Anti-Tank (D10 + Pen) against the structure's ARM for EACH hit.

8. OPERATIONS LOGISTICS

Objectives are captured by spending 1 AP (Special Action) while in control (within 3" with no enemy present).

MOMENTUM SHIFTS:
- CLAIM NEUTRAL: +2 MOMENTUM
- STEAL ENEMY: +2 TO SELF / -2 TO ENEMY

9. MISSION FILES

OP 01: FRONTLINE CLASH

Standard meeting engagement. Symmetrical objectives along the center axis.

  • Objectives: Center point and 12" inward from left/right edges.
  • No Man's Land: Claiming the center objective triggers 1 Stress.
  • Attrition: Turns 3-5: War of Attrition rule applies.

OP 02: SECURE THE SECTOR

Corner deployment scramble to lock down tactical quadrants.

  • Objectives: One in the center of each table quarter.
  • Deep Strike: Claiming enemy quarter objective grants +3/-3 shift.

OP 03: BREAKTHROUGH

Armored column attempt to punch through defensive lines.

  • Evacuation: Units exiting grant Attacker +2 / Defender -1 Momentum. (Max 3).

OP 04: HOLD THE LINE

Garrison forces defend intel from an encircling assault.

  • Extraction: Extract via 1 AP Special Action (+4/-4 Momentum shift).

OP 05: FIREFIGHT

Short-edge patrol engagement. High density combat.

  • Objectives: Center and 12" from long table edges.

OP 06: RAID

Strike against supply caches. Sabotage and extract.

  • Sabotage: Rigging (+3/-3 shift) costs 1 AP Special.
